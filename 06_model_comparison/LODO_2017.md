# Leave-one-day-out, BCCC-CIC-IDS-2017 (DT, RF, GNB)

Train on four capture days (600k stratified subsample), test on the whole fifth day. The held-out day's attack classes never appear in training, so scoring is binary: **detect** = share of attack rows predicted as any attack class; **FPR** = share of benign rows predicted as any attack class. Conditions: leaky model as is; same model with leaked timestamps +8 years or zeroed (no retrain); fixA = retrained with leaked IAT cells zeroed in train and test.
